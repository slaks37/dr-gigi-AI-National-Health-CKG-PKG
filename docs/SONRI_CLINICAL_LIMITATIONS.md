# SONRI Clinical Limitations

Updated 2026-10-16. Research implementation; no clinical or production approval.

Photographs cannot establish periodontal pocket/attachment/bone loss, pulpal vitality, caries depth, impaction, internal root disease, malignancy, histopathology or radiographic pathology. Local detector classes are raw Caries/Cavity/Crack/Tooth; no clinically approved ontology mapping is installed.

No validated oral-region rejection model, tooth orientation/FDI model, segmentation model, radiography model, calibrated medical confidence, or independent clinical benchmark exists here. Image-quality thresholds are engineering heuristics, not clinical validation. A normal-looking photo or absent detection is never proof of health.

Modules 2–5 have structured architecture/evidence workflows but no AI disease detector. Module 1 has local experimental inference only. Dentist-led protocol and independent validation are mandatory before configured clinical use. Generative AI is not required and cannot override triage or confirm diagnosis.
