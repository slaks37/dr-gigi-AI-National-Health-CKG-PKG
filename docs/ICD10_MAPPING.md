# WHO ICD-10 terminology and examination workflows

SONRI uses **WHO ICD-10 2010** for new screening, review, catalog and FHIR mapping. The official [SATUSEHAT ICD-10 documentation](https://satusehat.kemkes.go.id/platform/docs/id/terminology/icd/icd-10/) explicitly specifies version 2010 (verified 24 October 2026). This supersedes this checkout's earlier 2019 catalog. It does not migrate historical diagnoses automatically.

`src/clinical-engine/terminology/who-icd10-2010.json` contains the complete K00–K14 block: **123 entries: 15 categories and 108 terminal codes**. Titles and inclusion/exclusion/reference notes were extracted from the [WHO 2010 GetConcept endpoint](https://icd.who.int/browse10/2010/en/GetConcept?ConceptId=K00-K14). The original WHO response is retained at `src/clinical-engine/terminology/sources/who-icd10-2010-k00-k14.html`. Its SHA-256 is `12c27b0f68f7649ca9862732b98f47b1e4b4b16fcacb826a72c5c2f220f9a07f`; the catalog records source, date, system and edition. Tests compare all source title/code pairs against the shipped catalog. The original HTML remains the reference when flattened rubric text loses layout.

Only exact terminal codes may be confirmed. Category-only codes, duplicate codes, malformed codes and ICD-10-CM additions are rejected. K02.5 is absent from the 2010 catalog; WHO introduced it in 2013 ([official updates](https://www.who.int/docs/default-source/classification/icd/official-updates/official-who-updates-combined-1996-2012-volume-1.pdf)). It must not be silently mapped to another code. Historical records using a different edition require clinician re-review before a current FHIR preview.

`workflows.ts` defines authored research checklists for every category, with explicit per-code exceptions. `catalog.ts` combines these with the official terminology. Every entry exposes clinical evidence, potential observable findings, direct examination, conditional imaging/laboratory/pathology evaluation, unconfirmed differential codes, diagnostic limitations, source links, and AI/clinical validation status. These workflow fields are **SONRI-authored, pending clinician approval**; WHO provides classification, not these diagnostic requirements. Biopsy and imaging are conditional where appropriate, not instructions for automatic investigation. The model-support fields refer to clinically validated diagnosis capability; research inference availability does not change them.

`evaluateCodeEvidence(code, evidence)` reports missing checklist evidence or `CLINICIAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED`. A completed checklist is not proof that the examination result supports the disease; the helper always returns `confirmation_allowed: false` and `diagnostic_evidence_status: NOT_ASSESSED`. The authorized independent clinician review endpoint remains the sole diagnosis-confirmation path. `GET /api/clinical-catalog?category=K04` or `?code=K04.0` exposes the catalog to authenticated users without changing patient records.

Clinical rules retain observations, suspicions, differentials and confirmation as separate fields. Candidate `evidence_refs` identify only actual supported finding IDs and positive symptom answers; `missing_evidence` records what is absent. Differential diagnoses are alternatives, not copies of the primary candidate. Uncertain visual findings do not activate supported-finding rules. Visible cavitation may support an unconfirmed K02.9 suggestion; no photograph determines caries depth, pulpal status, periodontal stage or malignancy. No clinically validated disease model or clinical performance result is claimed.

Weak folder audit examples: `K0.2.3` is malformed; K02.2 means caries of cementum; K08.3 means retained dental root. Root remnants under K04.8 require review; acute avulsion under K08.1 requires injury/context review; K12.2 requires site/origin review. Filenames never become confirmed diagnoses.

Supporting examination references: [AAE endodontic diagnosis](https://www.aae.org/specialty/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2023/12/Fall2013-EndoDiagnosis.pdf), [EFP clinical periodontal decision tree](https://www.efp.org/fileadmin/uploads/efp/Documents/Campaigns/New_Classification/Guidance_Notes/report-02b.pdf), and [NHS persistent mouth ulcer assessment](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/mouth-ulcers/). Clinical owners must approve locally applicable diagnostic scope and pathways before production use.
